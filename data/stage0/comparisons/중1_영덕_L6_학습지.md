## 3. 문법 범위 — 학교가 정리한 그대로

### to부정사 — 명사 역할 (20쪽 · 25쪽)

| 항목 | 학교 예문 |
|---|---|
| to부정사만 목적어로 취하는 동사 | **want, hope, decide, plan, choose, need** — I want to play soccer. / She hopes to become a doctor. / We plan to watch a movie tonight. |
| 주어 (단수 동사) | To watch dramas is fun. / To help others brings happiness. / Wearing… → To wear a helmet can save your life. |
| 목적어 | They decided to clean the room. / Kate wanted to write a book. |
| 보어 (주어 = 보어) | Our goal is to improve our English. / My dream is to write a book. / Their plan is to go on a trip next summer. |

25쪽 Exercise A 오답·함정: `She loves play the piano` · `They plan joining` · `Did she finish to washing` — 그리고 **전치사 to 와 구별**
(`kind to everyone` · `from 9 to 6` · `went to Paris` · `walked to school` vs `decided to go`).
`finished / enjoy` 는 to부정사를 못 받는다 (2·7번).

7과 영일이 「to부정사 3용법 + 동명사 목적어」를 다룬 것과 달리, 영덕 6과는 **명사적 용법(주·목·보)만** 다룬다.
부사적 용법은 7과 (교과서 순서대로).

### 접속사 that (21쪽 · 26쪽)

| 항목 | 학교 예문 |
|---|---|
| 구 vs 절 | Jack is standing next to Minji. (구) / I believe that you are a genius. (절) |
| that절 = 목적어 · 보어 | We know that the sun rises in the east. / Good news is that Bryan finally got a job. |
| 동사 뒤 that 생략 | I believe (that) Mr. Kim is the best teacher. / We think (that) he is lying. / I guess (that)… / They heard (that)… |
| 지시사·형용사 that 과 구별 | That is my favorite book. / Can you believe that story? / that car over there / That jacket / vs I hope **that** he will get better soon. |
| 생략 불가 | 지시사 that (that movie · that today is…) — 26쪽 7번 |

## 4. 학교 문항 그릇 (25~26쪽)

L5 와 같은 **단문 5지선다 + 서답형**. 새로 보인 형식:

| 형식 | 예 |
|---|---|
| 밑줄 친 부분의 **역할에 ✓** (주어/목적어/보어) | I like to read books in my free time. |
| 빈칸에 알맞지 **않은** 것 | We ___ to go camping this weekend. ① planned ② finished ③ hoped ④ wanted ⑤ decided |
| 밑줄 친 to 의 쓰임이 나머지와 **다른** 것 (전치사 vs to부정사) | kind to · from 9 to 6 · went to · decided to go · walked to |
| <보기>와 쓰임이 같은 것 | <보기> His hope is to have a pet. (보어) |
| 역할이 같은 것끼리 짝지어진 것 | to drive buses / To eat healthy… |
| 밑줄 대신 괄호 말로 **바꿔 쓸 수 없는** 것 | They didn't like to go to bed early. (enjoy) ← enjoy 는 to 불가 |
| 공통 빈칸 (that) | I think ___ swimming… / Do you guess ___ that answer… |
| 접속사 that 인 문장 고르기 · that 을 쓸 수 없는 것 · 생략할 수 없는 것 | |
| 우리말 → 빈칸 (that 절) | 그는 모든 것이 좋아질 거라고 생각한다. → He thinks that everything will be okay. |
