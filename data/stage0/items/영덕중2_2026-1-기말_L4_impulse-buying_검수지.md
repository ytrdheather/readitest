## 지문 (배부본 원문, 문장 번호 부여)

[1] Have you ever bought something without planning, just because it looked nice or fun?
[2] This is called impulse buying.
[3] It means buying things suddenly, without thinking carefully.
[4] People often make impulse buying decisions while shopping, both in stores and online.
[5] Stores place snacks, toys, or small items near the cashier.
[6] You probably want to buy them while you wait.
[7] Impulse buying can lead to regret.
[8] You may feel bad later because you didn't really need the item.
[9] Also, buying many small things over time can use a lot of your money.
[10] It can stop you from following your spending plan or saving for something important.
[11] Impulse buying not only wastes money but also makes managing your budget harder.
[12] To avoid impulse buying, make a shopping list before going to the store and try to follow it.
[13] You can also give yourself a short break before you decide.
[14] Sometimes, waiting a few minutes can help you with your decision.
[15] Being a smart shopper means thinking before buying.
[16] When you shop with a plan, you can save money and feel better about your choices.
