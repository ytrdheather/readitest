## 지문 (배부본 원문)

[1] Have you ever bought something without planning, just because it looked nice or fun? [2] This is called impulse buying. [3] It means buying things suddenly, without thinking carefully. [4] People often make impulse buying decisions while shopping, both in stores and online.

[5] Stores place snacks, toys, or small items near the cashier. [6] You probably want to buy them while you wait. [7] Impulse buying can lead to regret. [8] You may feel bad later because you didn't really need the item. [9] Also, buying many small things over time can use a lot of your money. [10] It can stop you from following your spending plan or saving for something important.

[11] Impulse buying not only wastes money but also makes managing your budget harder. [12] To avoid impulse buying, make a shopping list before going to the store and try to follow it. [13] You can also give yourself a short break before you decide. [14] Sometimes, waiting a few minutes can help you with your decision. [15] Being a smart shopper means thinking before buying. [16] When you shop with a plan, you can save money and feel better about your choices.

## 논리 구조 태깅 (신규 — 태깅 B)

어법 태깅(태깅 A, 20개)과 **별개의 축**입니다. 주제·어구추론·빈칸 문항은 전부 여기서 나옵니다.

| 문장 | 역할 | 연결 신호 |
|---|---|---|
| [1] | 도입 질문 (관심 환기) | Have you ever ~? |
| [2]–[3] | 정의 | This is called / It means |
| [4] | 범위 | both A and B |
| [5]–[6] | 원인 (외부 요인) | — |
| [7]–[8] | **부정적 결과 1** — 후회 | can lead to |
| [9]–[10] | **부정적 결과 2** — 낭비 | **Also** (추가) |
| [11] | **결과 요약·강조** | **not only ~ but also** |
| [12] | **해결책 1** — 목록 만들기 | **To avoid** (전환) |
| [13]–[14] | **해결책 2** — 시간 두기 | **also** / Sometimes |
| [15] | **주제문** | Being ~ means ~ (일반화) |
| [16] | **결론문** | **When** (조건) + 이익 |

**논리 방향**: 도입 → 정의 → 원인 → 부정적 결과(2회 누적) → 전환(To avoid) → 해결책(2) → 주제 → 결론
**빈칸 후보**: [15] 주제문, [16] 결론문, [11] 요약문 — 계획서 9장 「빈칸은 거의 항상 주제문 또는 결론문」

## Q4. 【어구추론】 밑줄 친 부분이 의미하는 바로 가장 적절한 것은?
